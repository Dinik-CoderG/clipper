Answers

Can I clip a YouTube video without downloading it?

Yes. Paste the link and Clipper pulls the full video for you. You never have to download or re-upload anything yourself.

Does it work with long videos and podcasts?

Yes. Full videos up to 3 hours work, including multi-hour podcasts. Clipper scans the whole thing for the best moments.

Does it work with Twitch and Kick too?

Yes. Paste a Twitch or Kick VOD link and the same flow works.

Does Clipper add captions automatically?

Yes. Every clip gets word-by-word captions burned in, and face-tracking keeps the speaker centered in the vertical frame.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. Clipper runs on your own machine. Set an Anthropic API key to have Claude choose the moments; without one, a built-in scorer picks them. No watermark either way.
